CASE FILE #2291 — DOCUMENT 3
WITNESS STATEMENT — OVERNIGHT CUSTODIAL STAFF
Name on file: R. Alvarez
Shift: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM
Interviewed by: Campus Security, following morning

"I do the second floor of the CS building every night starting around
1 AM. Last night was pretty normal until about 2 AM, when I saw someone
walking fast down the lab wing hallway. They were wearing a red hoodie
with the hood up, so I couldn't see their face clearly. They looked
like a student, medium height, could've been anyone honestly.

They walked straight to one of the lab rooms without stopping to check
room numbers, like they already knew exactly where they were going.
I didn't think anything of it at the time since students pull all-
nighters in that wing all the time, especially with a deadline week
going on. I want to say they went into one of the rooms near the end
of the hall, but I couldn't tell you which one for certain — could've
been 216, 218, or 220, they're all close together.

About twenty minutes later I saw someone leave the same hallway, still
in the red hoodie, walking quickly toward the side stairwell instead
of the main lobby. I didn't see them again after that.

I also remember seeing two other students in the building around that
time — one was in the study lounge on the first floor the whole night,
and another one came in with a guest badge, like a visitor sticker,
sometime close to 2 AM. Neither of them was wearing a hoodie that I
noticed."

END OF DOCUMENT 3
